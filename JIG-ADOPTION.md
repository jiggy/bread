## Apply the standard bargain

Personal use, qualifying organizational use, and genuine evaluation follow
sections 2–3 without registration. A company purchase covers its named Group's
ordinary operations. Sponsorship cancellation ends future coverage and benefits;
it preserves rights to covered releases. Jig's four paid price bands remain
$10, $100, $1,000 and $10,000 for annual update coverage under P-0.5.

A publisher can include Jig coverage in a paid product through section 6's 1%
agreement. Customers can operate and modify that product's supplied Jig runtime,
including adding Flows, irrespective of their organization's size. They do not
acquire unrelated deployments or company-wide coverage. Free supplies of Jig copies or customer Jig environments leave recipients to
their ordinary eligibility or other acquired rights; receiving only application
results creates no customer Jig obligation.

For example, assume an enrolled publisher sells a genuine $100 paid assistant
containing a specified Jig release to a large company, with no tax, refund or
other deduction. The publisher owes $1 when those receipts are received. The
company keeps covered delivered-copy rights if the publisher later closes.
Independent application source need not be disclosed; distributed Jig source
and modifications must be supplied. The customer cannot extract Jig to license
an unrelated deployment under that purchase.

Jig's proposed [contributor agreement](CONTRIBUTOR-AGREEMENT.md) supplies broad
prospective licensing permissions if properly accepted. It is independent of
Bread adoption and supplies no authority over unsubmitted modifications or
separately licensed work.
