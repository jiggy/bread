The details that matter to you

This page summarizes the proposal. The draft license contains the exact terms.

Who qualifies for free use or evaluation?

Personal use is for your own non-organizational purposes. Employment and commercial client work use the applicable organization's permission.

For ordinary organization use, assess the controlled Group, including its parent and controlled entities. Financial scale adds revenue and qualifying funding without double counting, normally over the preceding twelve months. Exactly US$1m is outside the free allowance.

Evaluation means assessing whether, where, or how to adopt the software. It has no fixed time limit. Routine business work is operational use, even on a developer's laptop. Developing a product before operational adoption is also free.

Read the financial calculation and reassessment rules, including the 90-day transition after losing free eligibility in good faith.

What happens when I cancel or stop renewing?

A direct purchase covers existing official releases and those published during its purchased update period, subject to disclosed exclusions. Cancellation or nonrenewal ends future release coverage after that period; acquired rights to covered releases remain, subject to the license.

Included coverage for delivered copies of a paid product is also perpetual. It stays within that product, including customization and repair. Extracting its component into a separate deployment needs applicable permission.

Hosted access and its included coverage last for the paid service term. They do not promise continuing service or an export for independent operation. That needs expressly included delivered-copy coverage or your own applicable permission.

How do paid services, free plans, and competing hosts work?

For a paid product or service, publisher enrollment can include customer coverage for 1% of covered receipts. Alternatively, recipients can supply their own applicable permission. Competing products and hosts can use the same published terms.

For a results-only application, customers need no separate license merely because the software runs in its backend. The operator needs applicable permission; ordinary operator licensing is an alternative to publisher enrollment. A service running customers' own programs, workflows, or systems supplies customer environments that need coverage. Configuring an application's existing features alone does not create such an environment.

Free products and free plans supplying copies or customer environments require recipients' own applicable permissions. The operator also needs its own permission. A paid plan does not cover free users, and no blanket-coverage purchase is offered for free products.

Each independent licensor has separate coverage and payment obligations. See the guide's distribution examples for dependencies, onward resale, and supply notices.

What must I share, and how do forks work?

When distributing covered software, provide recipients with retainable covered source, including modifications, and the required build instructions and notices. This obligation does not extend to independent application files. Private operation or hosting alone does not require publishing modifications.

Distributed fork modifications are included under the existing upstream permission: applicable permission fees still go upstream, with no second permission fee for those modifications. Incorporating uncovered upstream releases needs applicable permission. Independently licensed additions keep their separate terms.

Bread is source-available commercial licensing, not OSI-approved open source. See the source and patent terms.

Can I adopt Bread for my own project?

Bread is intended as an independently reusable model. Draft 0.6 is not effective or ready for production adoption. Legal-text reuse permissions and the Bread name policy are still proposed.

Adopters would identify the software and licensor, publish their direct prices and update periods, and provide purchase and publisher-enrollment channels while preserving the standard bargain. Read the adoption guide and publication prerequisites.

The Jig adoption example and Jig's proposed pricing example illustrate one adopter's choices. They are not universal Bread prices or live offers.
