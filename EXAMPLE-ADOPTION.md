**Illustrative only — not effective.** Lantern Tools and its Ledger Engine
are fictional. This example applies the [adoption guide](ADOPTION.md) without
adopting Bread for any real software, offering a purchase, or granting rights.
The identities, release records, contacts, prices and orders are teaching examples.

## Illustrative price schedule E-1

These fictional prices are neither Bread-wide prices nor prices for an existing
product. They demonstrate that an adopter may choose its own paid schedule.

| Permission | Illustrative price and coverage |
| --- | --- |
| Personal use and genuine evaluation | Free under section 2. |
| Qualifying business use | Free while consolidated Group Annual Revenue is below US$1 million under section 3. |
| Company purchase | US$120 for a twelve-calendar-month Update Term, for any Group requiring a purchase. Taxes are additional and stated before purchase. |

One purchase covers the named Group across its Ledger Engine applications and
installations. It includes existing official releases and those first published
during the term. Their use and permitted modifications remain covered after
nonrenewal; adopting newer uncovered official releases needs applicable permission.
Growth alone does not reprice acquired rights. No publication schedule is promised.

## Joe's application and Northstar's company coverage

Joe sells a US$100 inventory application containing Ledger Engine to Northstar.
Northstar can use its already-acquired engine coverage if the included release
is covered. Otherwise, Northstar can buy directly or authorize Joe to arrange
the same US$120 company purchase naming Northstar.

Joe may advance US$120 to Lantern and recover it alongside his application
price. Lantern sends Northstar its actual coverage confirmation directly.
Paying Joe alone does not purchase an upstream license; he must disclose that
before collecting an upstream charge. His own license does not cover Northstar.
Once properly acquired, Northstar's grant survives Joe's business failure.

A second application using a covered Ledger Engine release needs no second
engine purchase. A second application needing an official release first published
after Northstar's term may require another purchase. Independent later repairs
to covered source follow section 7 without requiring renewed official coverage.

Free and paid distributions carry the same license, release, source and notice
requirements. Recipients use their applicable permissions. Joe need not audit
customers, report revenue, or enroll his product. Distributed engine source and
modifications are supplied; separate independent app files can remain private.

## Hosting and continuity

Joe's provider-controlled service uses Joe's permission, including free plans
and supplied browser execution. Northstar needs no separate engine purchase
merely for service access. If Joe instead administers Northstar's controlled
installation, Northstar's permission covers that work. An exported copy operated
independently needs the recipient's applicable permission.

If Northstar cannot purchase from Lantern for the documented 180-day period,
section 8's continuation grant can cover the engine releases already published
under Bread when Northstar's qualifying request was sent. The grant preserves
source and downstream company conditions, covers no future releases, and survives
resumed sales. This is not permission to operate during the waiting period.
