# Jig: use it, build with it, fund its development

**Review draft — not a live offer.** Proposed schedule P-0.5. The [license](LICENSE.md) contains the authoritative permissions; this page explains them. Its fee table becomes operative only when adopted and identified in an accepted order.

Jig is one complete product. Personal use and ordinary use by organizations below $1 million annual financial scale are free. Evaluation is free for everyone, with no registration or countdown. Assessing Jig is evaluation; adopting it for routine business work requires ordinary or included product permission. Employer policies still apply.

## Run Jig for your organization

Financial scale is your controlled Group's revenue plus qualifying funding, without double counting; the license defines the period and calculation.

| Annual financial scale, USD | Twelve months of update coverage |
| --- | ---: |
| Below $1 million | Free ordinary use |
| $1 million–under $10 million | $10 |
| $10 million–under $100 million | $100 |
| $100 million–under $1 billion | $1,000 |
| $1 billion or more | $10,000 |

Starting at $1 million, each tenfold band increases the fee tenfold, up to the $10,000 launch maximum. Applicable taxes are additional and shown before purchase. Self-declare the band; no routine accounts upload.

An annual purchase covers the Group's ordinary use of existing official releases and releases published during the next twelve months; any existing excluded material must be identified before purchase. Those release rights remain after renewal stops. A company sponsorship can acquire the same rights one paid month at a time. Its licensing portion is the annual fee divided by twelve, rounded up to the nearest cent; any additional support and benefits are displayed before purchase. There is no hidden minimum support contribution. An individual contribution does not cover an employer. Support-only sponsorship adds no release coverage and can accompany an existing license without paying for the same coverage twice.

Future prices may change with the license's notice and annual-review rules. They cannot change a paid period or revoke covered-release rights. There are no seat, Agent, execution, or machine charges.

Coverage includes subsidiaries later acquired by the named parent. A departing entity retains its purchased update term and covered-release rights for its own operations, without covering its new parent's other operations.

## Sell a product with Jig licensing included

Accept the published publisher agreement, identify your offering, and pay **1% of its covered receipts**. No negotiation, upfront charge, minimum payment, or cloud premium. Your customers receive coverage for that product or hosted service, regardless of their organization's size. They do not receive a license for unrelated Jig deployments.

The agreement defines taxes, refunds, pass-through compute, bundles, and reporting. Report aggregate receipts quarterly; no customer roster or runtime telemetry. The rate stays fixed for the enrolled product and its normal updates, and official Jig releases remain included while enrollment continues. A publisher stopping business does not erase a customer's acquired rights to delivered releases.

For a free product or free plan, users follow ordinary Jig eligibility; operating its free plan also requires the publisher's ordinary permission. There is no blanket-coverage purchase for free products. A paid plan does not cover all free users.

Previously acquired paid-product release rights remain valid. Issuing new coverage requires active enrollment, even if prepaid. Repairs and updates relying solely on the customer's existing covered release need no new coverage; existing hosting periods may use releases covered before enrollment ended. Publishers may also distribute with clear notice that recipients supply their own Jig entitlement.

Running and modifying the purchased product, adding Flows, and repairing its Jig component are covered. Extracting Jig to establish a separate deployment is not. Source must be available for distributed Jig and its changes; independent application code may remain private.

## Use a hosted application

Receiving answers or results from a hosted app does not require a Jig purchase merely because Jig runs in its backend. Its operator needs coverage. A service running your own Flows or systems supplies a customer Jig environment, even if the provider manages execution; merely configuring its existing app features does not. The provider can include permission with a paid service; a free environment follows ordinary eligibility. Generic VM rental supplies no Jig license. Independent operation of an export needs included delivered-copy coverage or your own applicable Jig permission.

## Your work stays yours

No Jig royalty merely for outputs or independently authored Flows. No activation server, compulsory licensing telemetry, or remote shutdown. FLOW and Sley remain independently licensed. Jig's custom license is source-available, not OSI-approved open source.
