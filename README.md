# Bread License

**A source-available licensing model with broad free access, clear commercial
terms, and lasting rights to covered releases.**

Bread serves maintainers who want to earn from their software and organizations
that need dependable permissions to build with it. Its core idea is reciprocity:
useful work, meaningful access, and clear commitments on both sides.

**Bread** names the initiative and reusable model; **Bread License** names its
legal instrument. Jig is the first intended adopter.

To assess adoption, read the [license](LICENSE.md) and [adoption guide](ADOPTION.md),
then the [Jig worked example](JIG-ADOPTION.md). The [doctrine](DOCTRINE.md) governs
the model's design, principles, and stewardship.

These drafts are not issued software licenses, executed agreements, or a live
purchasing offer. Extraction does not relicense Jig, FLOW, or Sley. Professional
review, actual adopter authority and operations, and the proposed text-reuse
and name policy remain prerequisites to stable public adoption. Doctrine guides
revisions; applicable legal grants determine actual permissions.

The independent repository contains the current working materials. Its
operation and editing guidance are in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).
